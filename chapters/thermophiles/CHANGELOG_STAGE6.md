## Verification and remaining uncertainty

The added bibliography records were checked against the primary article metadata: Kozubal et al., *Frontiers in Microbiology* 3:109 (2012), DOI `10.3389/fmicb.2012.00109`; Yang et al., *Molecular Microbiology* 77:1111–1122 (2010), DOI `10.1111/j.1365-2958.2010.07275.x`. The Beowulf value is an estimated field concentration-change rate (0.1–0.2 µmol L⁻¹ s⁻¹), not a rate attributed to one lineage. The 122 °C result remains an experimental upper-growth reference, not a theoretical limit. Reverse gyrase's full cellular benefit and the contribution of particular environmental populations to measured oxidation remain open scientific questions.
