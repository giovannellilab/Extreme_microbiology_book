# Scientific review and revision

## Purpose

Independently test the complete chapter for scientific accuracy, then correct the identified problems.

The scientific reviewer should not simply reproduce the drafting agent's reasoning.

## Review

Check the complete chapter against the evidence and cited literature.

Focus on:

- factual errors;
- outdated claims;
- unsupported generalisations;
- claim-citation mismatches;
- quantitative errors;
- taxonomy and nomenclature;
- incorrect mechanisms;
- overstatement of evidence;
- confusion between detection, survival, activity, growth and reproduction;
- missing caveats that materially affect interpretation.

Do not recommend additions merely to make the chapter more comprehensive.

Judge the chapter against its approved Gate 1 purpose.

Create:

`chapters/NN_chapter_slug/SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW.md`

Keep it concise.

For actionable findings, state:

- location;
- problem;
- required correction.

Finish with:

Scientific quality:
- Excellent
- Good
- Adequate
- Unsatisfactory

Publication readiness:
- Ready
- Minor revision
- Major revision
- Reject

## Revision

After the independent review, revise the chapter to resolve the findings.

Use targeted corrections.

Do not rebuild the chapter unnecessarily.

If a required correction would materially change the approved Gate 1 architecture, stop and return that issue to the editor.

Otherwise continue directly to the next workflow step.
