# Editorial, figures and integration

## Purpose

Turn the scientifically revised chapter into the complete reader-facing chapter that will be validated for Gate 2.

## Editorial pass

Improve:

- clarity;
- flow;
- accessibility;
- concision;
- transitions;
- consistency with the rest of the book.

Remove:

- generic filler;
- unnecessary repetition;
- review-article accumulation;
- unnecessary headings;
- material outside the approved scope.

Do not alter scientific meaning for stylistic reasons.

Preserve the author's teaching logic and distinctive examples.

## Figures and tables

Produce the figures and tables approved in `GATE1_PLAN.md`.

Follow:

`FIGURE_STYLE.md`

A final figure must:

- answer a clear scientific question;
- be scientifically checked;
- use the final production filename;
- include alt text;
- include a complete caption;
- be called out in the prose;
- render legibly at publication size.

Final figure assets belong in:

`book/figures/`

Do not leave ambiguous competing versions in the publication directory.

If the evidence shows that a planned figure is scientifically inappropriate, replace or modify it while preserving the teaching purpose defined at Gate 1.

## Integration

Before validation:

- integrate final figures and tables;
- resolve citations;
- remove placeholders;
- check chapter number and title;
- check internal cross-references;
- check figure and table numbering and callouts;
- ensure Further Reading is complete.

The result should be the complete chapter intended for Gate 2 review.
