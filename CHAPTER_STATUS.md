## Recorded ambiguities and production clarifications

- Chapter 17 sets `teachbooks_rendered: true`, and a chapter PDF exists, but the current public `book/_toc.yml` does not list the chapter. The table therefore records it as not published; this metadata discrepancy requires an explicit editorial decision and was not silently resolved.
- Chapter 18's three final publication assets are the files referenced by the chapter. The explicitly named `_source` and `_source_base` companions are retained only as editable production sources, not as competing publication candidates.
- The current public TOC is still the TeachBooks template structure and is not evidence that any numbered chapter has publication approval.
